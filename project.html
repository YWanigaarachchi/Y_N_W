Decoupled Architecture

Local Food Modifiers

GitHub

Athithi is a modern, flexible, and robust Hotel Management System tailored for the Sri Lankan hospitality industry. Built to handle everything from boutique villas in Ella to luxury resorts in Down South. Features include room booking, inventory tracking, local billing/tax compliance, and seamless guest management.

Key Features
Room Booking & Management
Inventory Tracking
Local Billing & Tax Compliance
Seamless Guest Management
Flexible for Villas to Resorts
Robust Secure Architecture
